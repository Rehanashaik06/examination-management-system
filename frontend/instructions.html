Important Instructions

  • The examination is timed. It will auto-submit when the timer reaches zero.
  • Each question has four options. Select only one option per question.
  • You can navigate between questions using Save & Next, Previous, or the question palette.
  • Use Mark for Review & Next to flag a question you wish to revisit.
  • Use Clear Response to remove a selected answer.
  • You may switch between subjects at any time without losing your answers.
  • Click Submit Test when you are done. You will see a confirmation before final submission.
  • Once submitted, you cannot change your answers.
